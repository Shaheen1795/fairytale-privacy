# WordWeave Support

Welcome to WordWeave: Crosswords and Ladders. Find help with playing, unlocking collections, and recovering purchase access.

## Getting started

Choose **Crossword** or **Ladder** to begin. Open **Game settings** using the sliders button to choose a collection, difficulty, and challenge size. Tap **Play** to apply your choices. Use **New board** to start a fresh challenge.

The free collection includes 1,000 words. You can play without creating a WordWeave account.

## Playing crosswords

Select a word using its numbered Across or Down button, or tap an unsolved word on the grid. Tap the letter tiles or type your answer, then tap **Check**. After solving a word, tap **Next word** to continue.

- **Shuffle** rearranges the available letter tiles.
- **Clear** clears your current answer.
- **Hint** helps reveal the answer. Using hints reduces the XP earned for that word.

In **Complete board** mode, find 10 connected words by tapping adjacent letters in a straight line. You can select letters forward or backward. Use **Clear selection** to begin a new selection.

## Playing word ladders

Connect the starting word to the target word. For each step, change, add, or remove exactly one letter. Every submitted word must belong to your selected collection. Enter your next word and tap **Check**.

## Difficulty and progress

Choose Easy, Medium, Hard, or Adaptive in Game settings. Adaptive difficulty responds to recent hints and unsuccessful checks.

The **Progress** tab shows XP, solved words, accuracy, and recognition time. Progress and saved challenges are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide account-based progress syncing between devices. Deleting the app may remove this local data. Restoring purchases restores eligible collection access, not gameplay history.

## Unlocking word collections

Open **Word vault** to view available purchases:

| Purchase | Includes |
| --- | --- |
| Collection 2 | 4,283 additional words |
| Collection 3 | 4,283 additional words |
| Complete Vault | Both paid collections: 8,566 additional words |

These are permanent, one-time purchases, not subscriptions. The Complete Vault includes the same content as Collection 2 and Collection 3 together. Review your existing purchases before buying a bundle.

Tap **Unlock** beside a collection to open Apple's purchase confirmation. The app shows the localized price supplied by the App Store. After Apple confirms the purchase, open Game settings and select the unlocked collection.

If a payment requires approval, access remains locked until Apple confirms it. Cancelling a purchase does not unlock the collection.

## Restoring purchases

1. Sign in to the App Store with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
2. Connect to the internet and open WordWeave.
3. Open **Word vault** and tap **Restore purchases**.
4. After restoration, select your collection in Game settings.

Google Play purchases do not unlock collections in the iOS app. Purchases refunded or revoked by Apple no longer provide access.

## Troubleshooting

### Prices or purchase buttons are unavailable

Check your internet connection, then open Word vault and tap **Retry prices**. Confirm that you are signed in to the App Store and that purchases are allowed on your device. If the issue continues, close and reopen the app and try again later.

### I paid, but my collection is locked

Check whether Apple shows the purchase as completed or pending. If completed, confirm that you are using the original purchasing Apple Account, then use **Restore purchases** in Word vault. If approval is pending, wait for confirmation before trying to buy again.

### Definitions do not load

Definitions use online dictionary services. Check your connection and tap **Retry definition**. Previously cached definitions may be available offline. Source and license links appear with available definitions.

### The app freezes or a challenge does not load

Close and reopen the app, then retry. If available, use **Retry** on the error message. Starting a new board replaces the current challenge for that configuration. If the problem continues, contact support before deleting the app so you can avoid losing locally saved progress.

### The board is larger than the visible area

Scroll to view the rest of the board and the controls beneath it. On iPad, rotating the device changes the available board width.

## Payments and refunds

Apple processes iOS purchases. For purchase history and refund requests, visit [Apple's Report a Problem website](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Apple determines refund eligibility.
