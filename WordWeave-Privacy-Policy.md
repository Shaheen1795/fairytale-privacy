# WordWeave Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WordWeave: Crosswords and Ladders for iPhone and iPad handles information when you use the app.

**Developer / data controller:** Shaheen 

**Privacy contact:** shaheen17995@gmail.com

## Information stored on your device

WordWeave stores your game settings, saved challenges, entered answers, solved words, hints, unsuccessful checks, time spent on challenges, XP, and progress history locally on your device. It also caches downloaded word definitions.

This information enables you to resume challenges, view progress, and use adaptive difficulty. The current iOS app does not upload your gameplay history to a developer-operated server or provide account-based progress syncing. You do not need to create a WordWeave account.

Device backups managed by Apple may include app data, depending on your device and backup settings.

## Online dictionary lookups

When a definition is not cached, WordWeave sends the selected vocabulary word to an external dictionary service. Lookups may happen automatically when a word is selected or a challenge is loaded. The app tries these services in order until a definition is available:

- Free Dictionary API: api.dictionaryapi.dev
- Datamuse: api.datamuse.com
- Wiktionary, operated by the Wikimedia Foundation: en.wiktionary.org

These services receive the requested word and ordinary connection information, including your IP address and request headers. The app sends an application-identifying User-Agent header. It does not include your progress history, purchase records, name, or email address in dictionary requests.

The services process requests under their own terms and privacy practices. Their retention periods are controlled by those providers. Requests are sent using HTTPS. Relevant provider information is available at [Free Dictionary API](https://dictionaryapi.dev/), [Datamuse API](https://www.datamuse.com/api/), and [Wikimedia's Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).

To prevent new online lookups, you can use the app without an internet connection. Definitions already cached on your device may remain available. Purchases and restoring purchases require a connection.

## In-app purchases

Apple processes payments for optional word collections through the App Store. WordWeave uses Apple's StoreKit framework to retrieve prices, request purchases, verify transactions, restore eligible purchases, and determine which collections you can access.

The app processes product identifiers and verified transaction information supplied by Apple, including ownership and revocation status. WordWeave does not receive your full payment card details or Apple Account password. It does not send purchase transactions to a developer-operated payment server.

Apple handles its own transaction records under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Deleting WordWeave does not delete your App Store purchase history or Apple's records.

## Advertising and analytics

The current iOS app does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs, use the advertising identifier, or track your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising. We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising.

Apple may collect or share app diagnostics according to your device's privacy and analytics settings and Apple's policies.

## Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, device details, and screenshots. We use it to respond, investigate problems, and maintain the app.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or payment card details. Remove unnecessary personal information from screenshots before sending them.

## Retention and deletion

Locally saved progress remains on your device until the app's data is removed. To remove it, delete the app using **Delete App** in iOS or iPadOS; offloading the app can retain its documents and data. Reinstalling or restoring a device backup may restore backed-up information. Manage backups separately through Apple's settings.

Definition caches may be removed by the operating system or when the app is deleted. WordWeave does not currently provide a separate in-app control to delete progress or dictionary caches.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request, maintain relevant support records, or meet legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support information. Information held independently by Apple or dictionary providers is subject to their retention and deletion processes.

## Your choices and rights

You can use the free collection without purchasing additional content, control network connectivity, manage Apple's diagnostics settings, and delete locally stored app data. There is no WordWeave account to delete.

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information we hold, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us at the privacy address above to make a request. We cannot directly access or recover gameplay data stored only on your device.

## Children

WordWeave does not require users to submit their name, age, or contact details to play. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support communications. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate. Apple's purchase permissions and parental controls apply to App Store transactions.

## Security and international processing

We limit the information the app sends and use HTTPS for dictionary requests. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Your device, Apple, dictionary providers, and email services used for support may process information in countries other than your own, under their applicable practices and safeguards.
